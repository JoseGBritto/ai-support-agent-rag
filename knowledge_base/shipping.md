# Shipping

## Argentina
We ship to every province in Argentina with Correo Argentino and Andreani. Orders placed before 1 p.m. (Argentina time) on a business day leave our roastery the same day.
Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days to major cities and up to 7 business days to remote areas.
Shipping is free on orders over ARS 60,000. Below that amount, shipping costs ARS 6,500.

## International
We ship to Chile, Uruguay, Brazil and the United States. Delivery to Chile and Uruguay takes 5 to 8 business days; Brazil and the United States take 8 to 14 business days.
International shipping costs USD 18 per order. Import duties and taxes in the destination country are paid by the customer.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order is dispatched. If the tracking has not updated for 5 business days, write to hola@andescoffee.example and we will open a claim with the carrier.
