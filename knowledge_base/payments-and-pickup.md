# Payments and pickup

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, Mercado Pago and bank transfer. Paying by bank transfer gives you a 10% discount. With a credit card you can pay in up to 3 interest-free installments.

## Pickup at the roastery
You can pick up your order for free at our roastery in Mendoza (San Martín 1234, Mendoza City), Monday to Friday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.

## Invoices
We issue type A and type B invoices (Argentine tax invoices). If you need a type A invoice, enter your CUIT at checkout.
