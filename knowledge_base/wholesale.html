Wholesale

We supply cafés, restaurants, hotels and offices in Argentina.

Wholesale prices

Wholesale customers get 25% off retail prices on orders of 5 kg or more per month, and 30% off from 20 kg per month.

Barista training

New wholesale partners receive a free 3-hour barista training session at their venue (Mendoza and Buenos Aires) or online.

How to apply

Send your business name, tax ID (CUIT) and estimated monthly volume to wholesale@andescoffee.example. We reply within 2 business days.
