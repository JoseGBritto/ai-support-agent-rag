# Products

## Coffee
All our coffee is specialty grade (84+ points), roasted every Monday and Thursday. We sell 250 g and 1 kg bags, whole bean or ground for espresso, moka pot, French press or filter.
Current origins: Colombia Huila (chocolate, red apple), Ethiopia Yirgacheffe (jasmine, lemon), Brazil Cerrado (nuts, caramel) and a house espresso blend.

## Subscription
The subscription delivers 1 or 2 bags every 2 or 4 weeks, with a 10% discount on every delivery. You can pause, skip or cancel at any time from your account, with no fees.

## Equipment
We sell manual brewing gear: V60 drippers, paper filters, AeroPress, French presses and burr hand grinders. We do not sell espresso machines.
