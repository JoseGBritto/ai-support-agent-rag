# Returns and refunds

## Unopened products
You can return unopened coffee bags and equipment within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The product must be in its original packaging.

## Opened coffee
Opened coffee bags cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. If you are not happy with the taste, tell us within 14 days and we will send you a different origin for free, once per customer.

## Damaged or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, send a photo to hola@andescoffee.example within 7 days. We replace it at no cost, including shipping.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. Mercado Pago refunds can take up to 10 days to appear.
